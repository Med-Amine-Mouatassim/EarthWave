HOW YOUR PLANET SOUNDS

Conditions become sound

Each setting changes a part of the synthesized soundscape. These are creative mappings to help you hear differences between environments.

🌱

Soil texture

Sandy soil adds a fine, airy grain; clay softens the high tones; rocky ground adds sharper texture; loam balances the layers; peat gives them a deeper, softer quality.

🪨

Rock type

Rock selection changes the resonant tone: granite and marble sound clearer, basalt sounds lower, limestone smoother, sandstone grainier, and slate crisper.

🌡️

Temperature

Temperature controls the main tone's pitch. Warmer settings raise it; colder settings lower it.

🌧️

Precipitation

Precipitation sets how often short, rain-like tones appear. More rain creates a denser pattern.

💧

Humidity

Humidity changes the filtering of the texture, making the sound softer and more blended as it rises.

🌬️

Wind speed

Wind adds a filtered, airy noise layer. Higher speeds make that layer more pronounced.

◉

Atmospheric pressure

Pressure shifts a quiet background tone, subtly changing the soundscape's sense of depth.
